Work Contract
This agreement (the "Contract") is made between Amazon Asia ("Party A" or "The Platform") and the Member ("Party B" or "You"), who agrees to participate in completing orders as part of the Amazon Asia Bronze Member Program. By signing this Contract, Party B acknowledges that they understand and agree to the terms and conditions outlined herein.

1. About Amazon Asia

Amazon Asia is an innovative platform designed to connect agents and members to facilitate the purchase and sale of products. Many of our agents have numerous buyers in regions where Amazon services are unavailable.

To efficiently address their needs, our platform employs a unique model:

  • Instead of buying large quantities of products directly—which incurs high import costs, delays, and tax complications—we enable members to place orders through their individual accounts on Amazon Asia.
  • By acting as separate merchants, members help minimize costs and speed up the delivery process.
  • It acts like AutoDs by assigning and fulfilling orders.

This system not only benefits the agents and their buyers but also creates earning opportunities for our members. It is essential that members fully understand their role and responsibilities within this system to ensure smooth operations.

2. Member Tasks and Responsibilities

2.1 Tasks Overview

As a Bronze Member, Party B is required to complete Two sets of task, each set comprising 2-3 orders. These tasks are assigned by the system, which evaluates overall task completion ratios by other members and determines the most suitable merchants requiring orders based on Party B’s balance.

  • Balance Matching: While the system attempts to assign tasks that match Party B’s balance, this may not always be possible. In such cases, tasks with greater requirements may be assigned to Party B if lower-balance tasks have already been allocated to other members.
  • Additional Deposit Requirement: If a task with greater requirements is assigned, Party B must deposit the additional required amount to complete the orders. This is mandatory, and completing these orders often results in higher profits compared to lower-balance tasks.
  • Delayed Deposit Consequences: If Party B delays depositing the additional required amount to complete the assigned orders within the given time, the merchants initially assigned to them may be replaced. In such cases, Party B will be assigned new merchants with greater requirements, potentially increasing the product purchase amounts. Delays in such situations will also negatively impact Party B’s credit score on the platform.

2.2 Completion Requirement

  • All assigned orders within the task set must be completed in full.
  • Failure to complete all assigned tasks will result in the forfeiture of Party B’s deposit for those orders.

2.3 Cancellation Policy

If Party B wishes to cancel the tasks before completion, the following conditions apply:

  • Party B must pay 30% of their balance and any pending amount related to incomplete orders.
  • Cancellation without proper completion is strongly discouraged, as it disrupts the workflow and may impact Party B’s standing on the platform.

2.4 Adherence to Guidance

  • Party B is required to follow instructions provided by Party A’s system.
  • Any issues arising from Party B’s failure to follow guidance or complete tasks will be their sole responsibility.

3. Platform Rules and Credit Score

  • Credit Score Importance: Party B’s credit score on the platform is a key metric that reflects their reliability and performance.
  • Impact of Non-Compliance: Failure to complete tasks, follow guidance, meet deadlines, or delay deposits for assigned orders will negatively impact Party B’s credit score, potentially limiting their future opportunities on the platform.

4. Deposit Policy

  • Party B’s deposit for assigned tasks will only be returned upon successful completion of all orders in the task set.
  • If the tasks are not completed, the deposit will not be refunded, as outlined in Section 2.2.

5. Profit Guarantee

Party A guarantees a profit of 15% - 30% on Party B’s deposit amount, provided Party B follows all guidance and completes the assigned tasks as per the terms of this Contract. If Party B fulfills all obligations and the profit does not meet the guaranteed range, Party B has the right to request compensation from Party A.

6. Legal Provisions

This agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of the United States of America. As Amazon Asia is a US-registered platform, this Contract and its terms are subject to oversight by the relevant US authorities responsible for business and consumer protection laws.

  • Any disputes arising from this Contract shall be resolved under the jurisdiction of US law.
  • Both Party A and Party B acknowledge that breaches of this Contract will be addressed in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations of the United Kingdom.

8. Acknowledgment and Agreement

  • By signing this Contract, Party B confirms that they:
  • Understand the purpose and structure of the Amazon Asia platform and their role within it.
  • Agree to complete the tasks assigned to them as described.
  • Accept the terms regarding deposit refunds, cancellation policies, and adherence to guidance.
  • Acknowledge the importance of maintaining a high credit score on the platform.
  • Failure to adhere to the terms of this Contract may result in penalties, including but not limited to, forfeiture of the deposit and suspension from the platform.

This Contract serves as a binding agreement between Party A and Party B. Both parties agree to uphold the terms and conditions herein to ensure a fair and productive partnership.
